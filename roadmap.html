Strategies

💡 6 Golden Rules for DSA Success

  1. Understand the Pattern, Don't Memorize Solutions: Most problems fall into ~10 core patterns (Two Pointers, Sliding Window, Fast & Slow Pointers, BFS/DFS, Top K Elements).
  2. Talk Out Loud: In interviews, communicate your thought process before writing code. State your initial brute-force approach first, then optimize.
  3. Always Analyze Complexity: After writing any solution, explicitly state its Time and Space complexity in Big O notation.
  4. Test Edge Cases: Check empty inputs ([], "", None), single-element inputs, negative numbers, and duplicates.
  5. Master Python Standard Library: Know when to use collections.deque, collections.Counter, collections.defaultdict, heapq, and bisect.
  6. Consistency over Intensity: Solving 2 problems a day for 4 weeks is far better than grinding 20 problems in 1 day.
